# Frontend Phase 7A — Customer Portal Foundation + Sample Request

Customer-facing only. No quotation acceptance, payment, sample approval/revision (7B), no orders/shipments/reorder (7C), no public website.

## Portal auth architecture and staff/customer isolation
- `lib/api/client.ts` was refactored into `createApiClient({ tokenStore, refreshUrl, onExpired })`. The staff client (`api`, `refreshAccessToken`) is unchanged; the customer gets its own instance (`lib/customer/client.ts` → `customerApi`).
- **Customer session state** (`lib/customer/session.ts`): in-memory access token, "session ended" signal and hint cookie name — nothing shared with the staff token store.
- **Cookies**: staff keep `bp_session` (hint) + `bp_rt`; the customer uses `bp_c_session` (JS-readable hint, path `/`) + `bp_c_rt` (httpOnly refresh token, path `/api/customer` — the browser sends it only to the customer auth routes).
- **Same-origin BFF routes**: `/api/customer/auth/{login,refresh,logout}` → backend `/customer/auth/*` (refresh token moved into the cookie; the browser only sees the access token; a rejected refresh clears the customer cookie, 429/5xx keep it). Public calls (activate, forgot-password, reset-password) go straight to the backend without a session.
- **Providers**: the staff `AuthProvider` moved from the root layout into the `(admin)` and `(auth)` layouts; `/account/*` has its own `CustomerAuthProvider`. A customer sign-out clears only the `["customer", …]` query cache and cookies; a staff sign-out never touches the customer's. Session restore uses only the customer hint cookie, retries on 429/5xx/network and signs out only on a definitive 401/403. Changing the password (backend revokes every session) signs the customer out and asks for the new password.
- Server-side gate: `/account/(portal)` redirects to `/account/login` without the customer hint cookie (never the staff one); the first API call validates the session.

## Routes
Public: `/account/login`, `/account/activate?token=`, `/account/forgot-password`, `/account/reset-password?token=`.
Portal (shell: Dashboard · My samples · Profile, no staff navigation): `/account`, `/account/samples` (server-side status/brand/search/sort/paging in the URL), `/account/samples/new`, `/account/samples/[id]` (tabs Overview · Specification · Files · Activity; `?submitted=1` confirmation), `/account/samples/[id]/edit?step=…`, `/account/profile`.

## Live contract used (`/customer/*`, verified from the live OpenAPI)
`auth/{login,refresh,logout,me,activate,forgot-password,reset-password,change-password}`, `profile` (GET/PATCH), `brands`, `addresses` (list/create/PATCH), `configurator/sample-options`, `terms/sample`, `samples` (list: `page,pageSize,status,search,brandId,sort`; create `{brandId}` only; GET/PATCH), `samples/:id/{decorations,accessories,components}` (add/PATCH/DELETE), `files` (multipart `file,type,description?,decorationId?`; list; DELETE; `download-url`), `submission-check`, `terms/accept`, `submit`, `activity`, `messages`. The customer is never sent as an id: ownership comes from the session; every call uses only the resource id.
Customer statuses (backend projection, rendered as sent — `statusLabel`, `nextStep`, `editable`): DRAFT, SUBMITTED, UNDER_REVIEW, QUOTATION_READY, PAYMENT_REQUIRED, IN_SAMPLE_PRODUCTION, AWAITING_CUSTOMER_APPROVAL, APPROVED, CHANGES_REQUESTED, CANCELLED (the frontend only picks badge colours).

## Dashboard and My samples
Greeting, **Request a sample** CTA, counters from the customer's own list (Active, Drafts, Waiting for you, In progress), and the five most recently updated samples (number, product, brand, label, nextStep). Drafts link to the editor ("Continue editing"), everything else to the detail. Empty states: "Request your first sample"; no brand → "No brand is linked to your account yet. Please contact Benue Print." (no brand creation — brands are read-only for customers). Waiting-for-you statuses are only *shown*; their actions are Phase 7B.

## Sample request (guided steps; `/account/samples/new` → draft → `/edit`)
`new` picks the brand and creates the draft (`POST /customer/samples {brandId}`), then the editor opens on **Product**. Steps: Brand · Product · Fabric & colour · Size & fit · Decoration · Accessories & labels · Artwork & files · Notes · Review & submit (stepper in the URL `?step=`).
- **Progressive saving**: each form step PATCHes only its own fields; **Save draft** works with incomplete data ("Draft saved"); leaving a step (Back / stepper / Continue) saves its edits first and stays put if the save fails. Decorations, accessories, labels/packaging and files are written immediately through their own endpoints. A submitted request is not editable (friendly message).
- **Product**: catalogue (`productTypeId`) or "something else" (`customProductName` + description); switching clears the other side with `null`.
- **Fabric & colour**: provider labels (Benue Print will supply / I will supply / Not decided yet — backend values stored), catalogue fabric (filtered to those allowed for the product, recommended first) or own fabric; colour from the fabric's swatches (name, hex, Pantone from the configurator) or custom (name, Pantone, optional `#RRGGBB`); a custom fabric forces custom colour fields; GSM optional with the help text "Fabric weight in grams per square metre" and the fabric's range.
- **Size & fit**: sizes from the configurator's scales; fit labels (Use a Benue Print pattern / Copy an existing garment / I have a size chart / I'm not sure yet); the pattern picker appears only for the Benue Print pattern fit and sends `patternVersionId` (nothing else about the pattern is shown); the size-chart fit points to the Files step.
- **Decoration**: zero or many placements (print method, placement, side, width/height + unit, colours, notes, "artwork needed"); **Accessories / labels & packaging**: configurator options (labels grouped by the backend category) or custom names.
- **Files**: types REFERENCE, ARTWORK, SIZE_CHART, OTHER; ARTWORK can be linked to a placement (`decorationId`, which creates a version of that placement's artwork under the hood — not shown); uploads go through the backend private pipeline; downloads request a short-lived signed link **on click only** (resolved against the API origin, never displayed); removal wording: "Removing a file takes it off this request. We may keep a record of earlier artwork versions."; only the customer's own uploads are listed (staff mockups are not visible — backend limitation).
- **Notes**: customer-facing notes and special instructions. `factoryInstructions` and every other internal field simply cannot be produced by the payload builders (tested).
- **Review & submit**: plain-language summary, **Sample Terms** (title, version, text; the customer ticks "I accept the Sample Terms (version N)" and `POST …/terms/accept {termsVersionId}` — authenticated customer acceptance, not staff-recorded), the backend **submission check** (`problems[]` shown verbatim as the checklist; nothing re-validated client-side), and **Submit my request** (enabled when `ready`; `POST …/submit`, never a status PATCH). Success → the sample page with "Request submitted. Our team will review your request." Backend refusals are shown and nothing is claimed as sent.
- The submitted sample enters the existing staff workflow unchanged (it appears in admin `/samples` as SUBMITTED and in the Command Center's *New requests*).

## Sample detail, activity, messages
Overview (number, brand, product, status, **What happens next**, dates), Specification (names only — no ids, no staff or internal fields), Files (own uploads), Activity (the backend's customer-safe timeline, newest first) and read-only "Messages from the team" when present. A missing **or foreign** id is the plain "Sample not found" (nothing about whether it exists).

## Profile
`GET/PATCH /customer/profile`: phone, WhatsApp, country, city, Instagram, preferred language (en/ar); name and login email are read-only ("managed by Benue Print"). Addresses: list, add, edit (ownership-safe routes). Change password: separate form (12–128 chars, lowercase + uppercase + digit); success signs the customer out (backend revokes all sessions).

## Privacy
Customer UI never renders internal notes, factory instructions, staff assignments, QC/blocker/rework data, payment state, audit data or staff names; it only renders what the customer projection returns. No admin navigation exists under `/account`.
